Our Mission

Fighting hunger and reducing waste, one meal at a time.

Our Story

Founded in 2026, FoodBridge began with a simple observation: local restaurants were throwing away perfectly good food while community members went hungry. We built this network to bridge that gap.

Our Vision

We envision a community where zero edible food goes to the landfill. Through transparent logistics and dedicated volunteers, we aim to make food redistribution seamless and localized.

Our Values

Dignity, sustainability, and community action. We believe that access to nutritious food is a fundamental human right, not a privilege.
