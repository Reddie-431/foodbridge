Our Core Initiatives

How we turn community generosity into daily meals across the city.

01

Fresh Harvest

We partner directly with local farms, grocery stores, and bakeries to rescue unsold fruits, vegetables, and baked goods before they spoil, ensuring nutritious ingredients reach community kitchens daily.

15+ Local Farm Partners • Daily Morning Pickups
02

Volunteer Action

Our trained network of drivers, sorters, and meal-prep teams operates 7 days a week. Using real-time dispatch alerts, volunteers claim nearby surplus pickups and deliver them within safe temperature windows.

450+ Active Volunteers • 2-Hour Shift Flexibility
03

Sustainable Waste

Every ton of rescued food prevents harmful methane emissions in landfills. We follow strict food-safety handling protocols and compost any inedible organic scraps with local urban gardens.

200+ Tons Diverted • Zero-Landfill Goal
04

Hopeful Distribution

Hot meals, fresh produce boxes, and hygiene kits are distributed with dignity through partner shelters, pop-up street pantries, and mobile vans—no paperwork or barriers required.

12 Distribution Hubs • Open to All
